Submit

Submit job steps

  1. Upload complete, draft genome file (*.fa, *.fna, *.fasta) or GenBank assembly accession

  2. Select a species (default: Automated identification)

  3. Leave your email address (optional)

  4. Click the "Submit" button

Search job ID step

  1. If you already have a Job ID (YYYYMMDD-**-**-****), simply enter it here and click "Search" to view the results
